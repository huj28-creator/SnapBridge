Privacy

ShotSkiff has no account service, cloud photo upload, advertising, or analytics.

Images and storage

Images transfer locally to the Mac. The Mac inbox retains up to 20 app-managed files; older files are permanently deleted. The native camera path sends the original JPEG bytes without resizing, recompression, or metadata stripping, so camera metadata—including location when the camera included it—may remain in received images. Screenshot files preserve their PNG bytes.

Permissions

Camera and Local Network permissions are used by the optional iPhone companion. The screenshot workflow reads only new screenshot candidates from the explicitly selected trusted iPhone through the Mac's Apple device connection. It does not import the existing photo library. The receiver replaces the Mac clipboard for each received image.

Diagnostics

Local timing records may contain image IDs, event names, timestamps, transfer sizes, and connection type, but not image content, pairing tokens, or Apple credentials. They remain on the Mac. No diagnostics are sent to us.
