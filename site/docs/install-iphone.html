OPTIONAL CAMERA WORKFLOW · 可选相机功能

Install the iPhone companion

This is optional. Automatic screenshot importing works without installing an iPhone app. The camera companion captures full-quality camera photos and transfers them over an authenticated encrypted local connection.

此伴侣应用为可选功能。自动接收系统截图不需要安装 iPhone 应用。相机伴侣可拍摄原始画质照片，并通过经过身份验证的加密本地连接传输。

Requirements and limits

  • iPhone with iOS 17 or later; Mac with macOS 14 or later.
  • Xcode and the user's own Apple Account added in Xcode Settings → Accounts. No paid Apple Developer Program membership is needed.
  • The app must be open to capture and send camera photos. Background or locked-screen camera capture is not promised.
  • Free Personal Team provisioning expires after seven days. Reinstall or refresh with the optional Mac helper before expiry. The helper is conditional and cannot silently approve iOS developer trust, Apple verification, or account prompts.

Setup

  1. Install and open the ShotSkiff Mac app, then open its setup panel and keep the pairing code visible.
  2. Clone the public repository and open `SnapBridge.xcodeproj` in Xcode.
  3. In Xcode, choose the `SnapBridgePhone` target, select your connected iPhone, set your own Personal Team under Signing & Capabilities, and allow Xcode to manage signing.
  4. Build and run the iPhone target. If prompted, enable Developer Mode and trust the developer profile in Settings.
  5. Open ShotSkiff on the iPhone, select your Mac, and enter its one-time pairing code. The two devices save their pairing and reconnect automatically when reachable.
  6. Allow camera and Local Network access. Keep the app open while capturing photos.

For guided installation and the optional renewal helper, follow the full English instructions or 中文安装说明. The helper needs the same Xcode account and signing identity and a reachable device; it is not a permanence guarantee.
